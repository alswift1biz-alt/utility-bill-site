Contact / About BillPredict

The BillPredict Decision Tools helps everyday households slow down before taking on recurring financial commitments. Our calculators are built around a simple idea: the approved payment is not always the affordable payment. Before opening a card, buying a vehicle, signing a mortgage, or absorbing another monthly bill, visitors can test realistic numbers and see how a decision may affect their budget.

Our tools focus on practical questions: What might my utility bill look like next month? How should I schedule credit card payments around paydays? What is the real monthly cost of a vehicle once insurance, fuel, and maintenance are included? How much home payment can I afford without becoming house poor?

We do not provide individualized financial, legal, tax, mortgage, or credit advice. Instead, we provide educational calculators and plain-language explanations that can help you prepare better questions for lenders, utility providers, housing professionals, and qualified advisers.
