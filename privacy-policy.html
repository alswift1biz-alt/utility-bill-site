Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026. The BillPredict Decision Tools provides educational calculators and financial planning content. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information may be used to operate the website, improve calculators, respond to messages, monitor security, measure content performance, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal financial information.

GDPR and privacy rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with privacy rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal information we hold about you. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise rights, contact us through our contact form.

Data retention and security

We retain contact messages only as long as reasonably needed for business, legal, or support purposes. No website can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable safeguards and recommend that visitors avoid sending sensitive personal or financial credentials by email.

Changes
